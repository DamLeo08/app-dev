# My Favorite Movie

## The Usual Suspects (1995)

**The Usual Suspects** is a crime mystery thriller film directed by **Bryan Singer**. The story follows five criminals who become involved in a complicated criminal operation after meeting during a police lineup. As the story unfolds, secrets, deception, and unexpected events make the movie more mysterious and exciting.

### Why I Like This Movie

I like this movie because of its storytelling, suspense, and unexpected plot twists. It keeps the audience guessing about what is really happening. I also enjoy how the story connects different events and characters in a surprising way.
